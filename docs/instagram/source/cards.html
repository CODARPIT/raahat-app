How it works · यह कैसे काम करता है

One photo to a ranked report in under a minute.

  1. A citizen sends a photoFrom the website or Telegram, in Hindi or English. Works on 2G.
  2. AI reads itNames the hazard, counts people, judges water depth, flags screenshots and recycled photos.
  3. Duplicates merge, faces blurThe same photo forwarded fifty times becomes one report. Every face is pixelated.
  4. Responders actA ranked queue on a live map, with every point of the score explained.

Read the band · रंग पहचानें

Two colours, one rule: open the link.

  • ✓
    Green band: verified. A control-room responder confirmed the report. Faces are pixelated, the place is rounded.
  • ✕
    Red band: false. The photo is old, fake or misleading. Please don't forward it as news.

During a floodबाढ़ के दौरान

✓ Do · करें

  • Move to higher ground or an upper floor at once.
  • Switch off electricity and gas at the main supply, if it is safe.
  • Drink only boiled or chlorinated water.
  • Anyone trapped? Call 112 and send a photo to Raahat.

✕ Don't · न करें

  • Walk or drive through moving water. Even knee-deep water can sweep away a person or a car.
  • Go near electric poles, fallen wires or transformers.
  • Let children play in or near floodwater.

Adapted from NDMA public guidance. Full list: raahat-kappa.vercel.app/safety

In an earthquakeभूकंप के समय

✓ During · दौरान

  • Drop, cover and hold on: get under a sturdy table and hold it until the shaking stops.
  • Stay away from windows, glass and heavy furniture.
  • Outside? Move to an open area away from buildings, trees and wires.

✓ After · बाद में

  • Expect aftershocks.
  • Leave damaged buildings carefully and don't go back in.
  • Report collapsed buildings and trapped people: call 112 and send a photo to Raahat.
